Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026 · Contact: selenayang@126.com

This page explains what HeyVoyo (heyvoyo.com) collects, why, and what choices you have. We keep it short and only collect what the features need.

Using the map without an account

You can use the map without signing up. Your progress and settings (for example interface language, learning language, collected stamps, game levels and saved words) are stored only in your own browser (local storage). We do not receive them, and you can delete them by clearing your browser's site data.

If you create an account

  • Email address and password — used only to sign you in and to send account emails (confirm your email, login links, password resets). Passwords are stored hashed by our login provider; we never see them. We do not send marketing emails.
  • Display name and profile — the name you choose and anything you add to your profile (such as languages, city or a short bio) is shown to other members.
  • What you post — messages, replies and voice messages you choose to share in clubs are stored so other members can see them. Private messages are visible only to you and the other person.
  • Follows — who you follow, so we can show their posts.
  • Synced learning data — when you are logged in, things like your island word books, saved words and map progress are copied to your account so they appear on your other devices.
  • Voice recordings — the microphone is used only when you press record. A recording leaves your device only if you choose to post it.

We use this data to run the features you use (lawful basis: performing our agreement with you). Account data is stored with our database and login provider, Supabase; account emails are delivered by Resend. Both act only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile, posts and synced data within 30 days, except where we must keep something by law.

AI features

Some features (such as practice conversations) send the text you type to Anthropic's Claude to generate a reply. We count how many AI requests each account makes per day to keep costs under control. We do not use your messages to train models.

Services that load content

To show the page, your browser connects to: Cloudflare (images), OpenFreeMap (street maps), Google Fonts (fonts), unpkg (map software), and YouTube or Bilibili when you play a video (YouTube videos use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain). These services receive your IP address as part of normal web traffic.

Affiliate links

Some product links go to shops such as Amazon. If you click one, that shop may set its own cookies to record that you came from HeyVoyo. See our affiliate disclosure.

No ads, no tracking analytics

HeyVoyo does not show adverts and does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Children

HeyVoyo is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent's or guardian's knowledge. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct, export or delete your account data at any time by emailing selenayang@126.com. If you are in the UK or EU you also have rights under the UK GDPR / GDPR, including the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk).

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell logged-in users on the site.
