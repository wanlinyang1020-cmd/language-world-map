About HeyVoyo

Last updated 24 October 2026

HeyVoyo is an interactive world map for language learners. You sail a little boat between stickers — places, foods, festivals, brands, everyday shops — and at each one you learn the words, sentences and short conversations you would actually need there.

It is built mainly for people learning Chinese, and also for people learning English. Topics are chosen from what people talk about now: daily life, health, money, travel, work and the wider world.

What you can do

  • Explore stickers on a world map and a street map, with words, example sentences and pronunciation.
  • Practise basic conversations for places found in every country: airports, metro stations, hospitals, restaurants, banks, cafés and more.
  • Look inside brands and learn the vocabulary for shopping there.
  • Join clubs to chat with other learners.
