How it works

Open. Step in. Say it.打开，走进去，说出口。Three small moves a day.

i.

每天打开一页Open a page each day

象牙色的封面，一天打开一次。里面是今天的三站：岛上的主题，或者开船去的真实城市。An ivory cover you open once a day. Inside: today's three stops — topics on the islands, or real cities you sail to.

ii.

走进真实的短片Step into a real clip

首尔中午的光化门、伦敦的市场、天刚亮的厨房——字幕就在画面上，先听，再跟着说。Noon in Seoul, a London market, a kitchen at dawn — subtitles on the picture. Listen first, then say it.

iii.

说出口，送给世界Say it, send it

说完盖一个邮戳。愿意的话，再用母语说一遍，送给正在学你母语的人。Each line gets a postmark. If you like, say it again in your own language for people learning it.

The map

Learn on the islands. Say it in the real world.岛上学，开船去真实的城市说。Islands at sea, real cities on land, your boat in between.

Today's World今天世界上这三个地方正在发生一件事，全世界看到的都是同一期。猜猜是什么，点照片看答案。Something is happening in these three places today — the same three for everyone. Guess what, then tap the photo.
Other voices

Your voice is someone's lesson.你的声音，是别人学的那一句。Every accent belongs here.

同一句话，听英国人说，也听法国人、印度人、中国人说。真实的世界里，大部分对话本来就发生在不同口音之间。Hear the same line from a Londoner, and from French, Indian and Chinese speakers too. In the real world, most conversations happen between accents.
